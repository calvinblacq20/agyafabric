(01) THE STORY

Agya Mensah sold cloth from a stall at Makola from 1974, and knew every weaver by name. His daughter moved the house to Osu in 2003 and kept the rule he traded on: pay the weaver in full, on collection, and never call a printed cloth a woven one.

SHOWN AT Chale Wote Street Art Festival · Accra Fashion Week · Kumasi Museum, loan collection · Design Week Lagos

WRITTEN ABOUT IN Graphic Showbiz · Accra Weekly · Selektah · The Continent

A NOTE ON PRICE Kente is expensive because it takes eleven weeks. If you find the same cloth for a fifth of the price, it came off a roller in a factory.
