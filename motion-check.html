On iOS prefers-reduced-motion is true for Low Power Mode as well as the Reduce Motion toggle, so a phone at 20% battery reports the same thing as one whose owner asked for less movement. That is why the site treats it as calm — fades and constant-rate loops keep running — rather than switching everything off.

Background this tab for a few seconds and come back: the gap figure should spike and then recover. If it never recovers, the watchdog is not doing its job.
